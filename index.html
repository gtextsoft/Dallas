The man in the room

Meet Dr. Stephen Akintayo.

Dr. Stephen Akintayo is the Chairman of SACI Holdings — a global private equity firm with interests in real estate, technology and emerging markets across Africa, the UAE, the UK and the USA. He is the Founder and President of the Stephen Akintayo Foundation, and the founder of Gtext Holdings, a multinational conglomerate he built from the ground up.

In 2023, Forbes named him the Best of Africa's Leading Investment Coach & Real Estate Mogul. He sits on the Forbes Business Council, has authored 45+ books on wealth and entrepreneurship, and hosts The Billionaire Habits Podcast. The part that matters most: he started with nothing — from a thrift-collection side hustle to a recharge-card stand in Abuja. His mission is bold and specific: to raise one million millionaires by 2050. On July 26, a few of them could be sitting in Dallas.
